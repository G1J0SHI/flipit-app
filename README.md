FlipIt is a minimal, tactile decision toolkit built for speed, clarity, and focus. Whether settling a debate, picking numbers, or letting chance decide, FlipIt gives you instant, unbiased tools right in your pocket.

Designed with functional minimalism: no ads, no accounts, and no distractions—just fluid physics, satisfying tactile feedback, and cryptographically fair randomness.

---

### ✨ FEATURES & HIGHLIGHTS

- **Tactile Decision Tools** - A growing pocket toolkit including multi-coin flips, physics-driven dice rolls, customizable random number draws, and more
- **Satisfying Motion & Physics** - Natural 3D rotation, elevation bounce, and reactive haptic and acoustic feedback on every interaction
- **Cryptographically Fair** - Built on secure pseudorandom generation (CSPRNG) to guarantee truly unbiased outcomes every time
- **Functional Minimalism** - Distraction-free, high-contrast monochrome design that feels natural in both Dark and Light modes
- **100% Offline & Private** - No internet required, no analytics, no tracking, and no permissions beyond vibration

Simple, fast, and fair. Make every decision effortless with FlipIt!
