# Privacy Policy

Our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document contains types of information that is collected and recorded by the Application and how we use it.

## Information Collection and Use

The Application is designed as a completely offline decision toolkit. It does not collect, record, or transmit any personal identifiable information, device identifiers, or analytics. All preferences and generated results remain strictly on your local device.

## Device Permissions

The Application requests only the `VIBRATE` permission, which is used solely to provide physical haptic feedback during user interactions (such as flipping coins, rolling dice, and generating numbers). No personal data or sensor tracking is accessed through this permission.

## Security

I value your trust in using our Application. Because no personal information is collected or transmitted over the internet, your information remains entirely within your control on your hardware.

## Links to Other Sites

This Application does not contain third-party advertisements or external tracking links. If you visit any external developer or store links, you are advised to review the Privacy Policy of those respective sites.

## Children’s Privacy

Another part of our priority is adding protection for children while using the internet. We encourage parents and guardians to observe, monitor and guide their online activity.

The Application does not knowingly collect any Personal Identifiable Information from children under the age of 13.
